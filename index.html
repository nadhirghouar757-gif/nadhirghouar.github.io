01. A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Language learning

I am a teacher of English with a Master's degree in Foreign Language Teaching and experience across university, academy and online learning environments.

My work brings together TEFL methodology, communicative instruction and curriculum design. I plan learning experiences around learners' levels, goals and contexts, from foundational English to more independent communication.

I am particularly interested in how thoughtful teaching, digital tools and well-designed materials can make language learning more accessible and engaging.
